# Skill Specification

The authoring and behavioral source of truth for skills in this repository. It follows the sibling skill repos (Productside Precedents Thinking, ADLC, Market Intelligence) and departs from them only where the keynote stage needs it; departures are marked.

`python3 scripts/check-library.py` enforces the mechanical parts. Behavior is checked by [the funnel cases](../evals/loop-cases/README.md), not by this script.

## Location

Every skill lives at `skills/<skill-name>/SKILL.md`.

`.claude/skills` is a symlink to `../skills`, so Claude Code discovers `/bdk-…` commands when this repository is the working folder (the keynote runs its demos this way). The plugin manifests in `.claude-plugin/` and `.codex-plugin/` point at the same `skills/` folder for installed use, where commands appear as `/bdk:bdk-…`.

## Three kinds of skill

| Kind | Names | Role | Frontmatter key |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Funnel skills** | the seven step skills, the two deep dives (`bdk-context-pantry`, `bdk-four-risks`), and `bdk-detector` | The Baloney Funnel: one step each, a deep dive, or the whole funnel (`bdk-detector`) | `tier` |
| **Utilities** | `bdk-util-*` | Feed an artifact to a named tier or take one from it | `supplements` |
| **Quick checks** | `bdk-value-or-theater`, `bdk-skill-sorter` | The keynote stage demos, outside the funnel | `family` |

**Departure from Precedents:** funnel skills are *not* named `bdk-stepN-…`. They keep their check names because they are demoed two or three at a time on a projector, where `/bdk-receipts` reads and `/bdk-step1-show-me-the-receipts` does not. Order comes from the `tier` field and the [catalog](../catalog/INDEX.md), not from alphabetical sorting.

### Tier values

| `tier` | Skills |
|---|---|
| `1-chaos` | step 1 `bdk-outcomes`, step 2 `bdk-receipts` |
| `2-confusion` | step 3 `bdk-labels`, step 4 `bdk-sell-by`, step 5 `bdk-relevance`; deep dive `bdk-context-pantry` |
| `3-crapola` | step 6 `bdk-taste-test` |
| `4-considered` | step 7 `bdk-pivot-punt-pursue`; deep dive `bdk-four-risks` |
| `funnel` | `bdk-detector` (runs all seven steps) |

The step order (outcome, receipts, labels, sell-by, relevance, taste test, pivot-punt-pursue) is set in the assessment contract and `bdk-detector`, not by the `tier` field.

### Utility rule

A `bdk-util-` skill earns its place only if it hands an artifact to a named tier or takes one from one.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Declares `supplements: <tier>`, never `tier` | It is beside the funnel, not a position in it |
| `depends-on` is empty | It is invoked on its own |
| The body says it does **not** continue into the funnel | Keeps the funnel four tiers long |
| **At most six utilities exist** | The supporting cast never outnumbers the method |

### Quick-check rule

Quick-check names are frozen: the keynote quotes `/bdk-skill-sorter` and `/bdk-value-or-theater` more than 80 times. Their output must stay readable on a projector from the back row. They keep the keynote's own section layout instead of the funnel sections below, and must still carry a worked and a weak example.

## Frontmatter

Author the rich canonical shape. The portable shape used in `dist/` ZIPs is generated by `scripts/build-distribution.py`: it keeps `name`, `description`, and `license` at the top level, moves every other key into a string-to-string `metadata` map, and generates `agents/openai.yaml` from the `interface` block. Do not hand-edit generated files.

```yaml
---
name: bdk-receipts
description: Trigger-oriented sentence(s) saying what it does and when to use it.
license: CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0
argument-hint: "[claims, links, or source list]"
intent: >-
  The richer job: who it serves, what goes wrong without it.
theme: baloney-detection
tier: 1-chaos                  # or supplements: 1-chaos (utilities), or family: work | claims (quick checks)
sift-question: "What evidence supports that outcome's metric and direction?"   # funnel skills only
status: active
best-for:
  - "Concrete use"
scenarios:
  - "Realistic trigger situation"
produces:
  - "Named artifact"
depends-on: []
combine-with:
  - bdk-sell-by
interface:
  display_name: "Show Me the Receipts"
  short_description: "25-64 character summary"
  brand_color: "#00E874"
  default_prompt: "Use $bdk-receipts to … and stop at …"
  allow_implicit_invocation: true
---
```

Rules:
- `name` matches the folder.
- `description` is trigger metadata, not marketing copy: what it does and when, under 1,024 characters (aim for under 250).
- `license` is always `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`.
- `interface.short_description` is 25 to 64 characters; `default_prompt` names `$<skill-name>` and says where it stops.
- `depends-on` is a list. No funnel skill requires an upstream artifact; earlier tiers are optional companions.

## Required files

```text
skills/<skill-name>/
├── SKILL.md
├── template.md                 # funnel skills and utilities; quick checks embed their output format
├── examples/
│   ├── worked-example.md       # synthetic, shows reasoning, not just a polished answer
│   └── weak-example.md         # synthetic anti-pattern: complete, tidy, wrong
└── references/
    └── assessment-contract.md  # generated copy; funnel skills and utilities only
```

Examples use fictional organizations and people only, never a real company or person, and they are marked synthetic.

## Required sections (funnel skills and utilities)

In this order:

1. `# Skill Name`
2. `## Purpose`
3. `## When to use it`
4. `## Inputs`
5. `## Key concepts`: at least three concepts, each ending with a `*Violation signal:*` line naming how a reader notices it being broken
6. `## Outputs`
7. `## Method`
8. `## Human decision gate`: the exits this skill can recommend, what it records, and that it stops
9. `## Evidence expectations`: links the bundled contract
10. `## Mode behavior`
11. `## Voice behavior`
12. `## Failure modes`
13. `## Assets and examples`: links `template.md` and both examples

Skill files teach in prose and tables. No mermaid diagrams under `skills/`; they cost tokens at inference and render as raw text in most skill viewers.

## Vocabulary

Use the house vocabulary exactly (see [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md)):
- **Five checks:** Show Me the Receipts · Label the Lunch Meat · Check the Context Pantry · Check the Sell-By Date · Taste Before You Ship
- **Funnel:** Chaos → Confusion → Crapola → Considered → Truth, in seven steps
- **Outcome statement:** [direction] [metric] [by how much] [by when] for [beneficiary persona] in [problem context], optionally without [a health metric] crossing [a limit] (the guardrail)
- **Solution hypothesis:** If we [change] for [beneficiary] then we will [outcome]; tested by tiny acts of discovery tagged by square; valid if within [timeframe] we observe a quantitative measure, a qualitative measure, and a hard stop
- **Exits:** Pass · Sifted out · Send back · Pivot
- **Four risks:** Valuable · Usable · Viable · Feasible, with Dean's questions verbatim
- **Moves for claims:** Pivot · Punt · Pursue
- **Moves for work:** Pass-off · Pivot · Punt · Pursue
- **Scale:** Real Deal · Mostly Meat · Real Imitation Baloney · Mystery Meat · Total Crapola
- **Modes:** Guided Discovery · Context Dump · Spitballing
- **Voices:** Product Coach · Comedian · Pirate · Boss · Antagonist

Funny is not mean. Punch up at hype, vendors, and weak logic; never at users, customers, affected workers, or people learning.
