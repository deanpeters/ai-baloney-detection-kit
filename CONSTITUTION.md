# CONSTITUTION.md

## The non-negotiables of the AI Baloney Detection Kit

The Constitution defines what this repo is for, what it values, and what it refuses to become.

Methods may evolve.

Tools may change.

Models certainly will.

These principles should remain stubborn.

---

## Article I — Evidence Beats Enthusiasm

Excitement is not evidence.

A charismatic demo, breathless keynote, leaderboard win, executive mandate, viral post, or vendor promise does not earn confidence by itself.

Interesting ideas still need receipts.

---

## Article II — No Claim Without Receipts

Claims that matter should be traceable.

Prefer original sources, authoritative systems, observable evidence, and reproducible methods.

When the source of truth should be deterministic, retrieve it deterministically.

Do not let generated recollection cosplay as a record.

---

## Article III — Label the Lunch Meat

Fact, inference, assumption, and best guess are not interchangeable.

Keep them visibly separate.

If we do not know, we say we do not know.

---

## Article IV — Context Before Conclusions

No AI idea exists in a vacuum.

Who is it for?

What problem are they trying to solve?

What outcome matters?

What constraints, risks, decisions, workflows, economics, and operating conditions shape the answer?

Thin context should reduce confidence, not increase creativity.

---

## Article V — Everything Perishable Gets a Sell-By Date

Markets move.

Models change.

Policies change.

Prices change.

Customers change.

Evidence ages.

Freshness is part of truthfulness.

---

## Article VI — Reality Gets the Final Vote

AI output is not validation.

A plausible answer is a hypothesis.

A polished prototype is a hypothesis.

A compelling strategy deck is a hypothesis.

Before we bet, we test whether the idea is valuable, usable, viable, and feasible.

---

## Article VII — Outcomes Over Outputs

Features are not strategy.

Shipping is not success.

Usage is not automatically value.

Automation is not automatically improvement.

The work should connect to meaningful outcomes for users, customers, teams, and the business.

That is why the Baloney Funnel starts by naming the outcome (`bdk-outcomes`): [direction] [metric] [by how much] [by when] for [beneficiary persona] in [problem context]. A pitch that is only an output, or only a vanity metric, stops there.

---

## Article VIII — Do Not Automate Waste Faster

Before adding AI, ask whether the work should exist at all.

Eliminate what does not matter.

Simplify what remains.

Then decide whether automation, augmentation, or AI actually improves the system.

---

## Article IX — Humans Remain Accountable

AI may assist, accelerate, augment, and amplify.

It does not absorb accountability.

For consequential decisions, someone must remain answerable for the outcome.

---

## Article X — Pedagogy and Practicality Are Peers

This repo should teach while it helps people do the work.

No laminated-framework theater.

No unexplained magic tricks.

No 47-step certification ritual before someone can pressure-test an idea.

Teach enough to improve judgment.

Do enough to create value.

---

## Article XI — Humor Lowers the Barrier, Not the Rigor

This repo should be fun to use.

Baloney, mystery meat, crapola, pirates, comedians, and other silliness are welcome.

But the joke must never replace the thinking.

Funny is not an excuse for sloppy evidence.

---

## Article XII — Product Thinking Before Technology Theater

Start with the person, problem, outcome, and economic or experiential value.

Do not begin with “we have an agent.”

Do not end with “therefore AI.”

Capability is an ingredient, not a strategy.

---

## Article XIII — Context Is Work

Good reasoning depends on good shared context.

Capturing relevant customer knowledge, decisions, constraints, source material, assumptions, definitions, and prior learning is part of the work.

It is not administrative overhead.

---

## Article XIV — Make Uncertainty Inspectable

The purpose of the kit is not to manufacture certainty.

It is to make uncertainty easier to see, discuss, test, and reduce.

A clear “not yet” is better than a confident pile of crapola.

---

## Article XV — Safe to Serve Must Be Earned

“Safe to Serve” does not mean risk-free or universally correct.

It means:

> **This bet, for these people, solving this problem, under these conditions, has earned the right to move forward.**

Nothing ships merely because it sounds delicious.

---

## The PM Code (the stage edition)

On stage, the Constitution is sworn in as **the PM Code**: three articles, one against each C. They are short forms of the articles above, not a second constitution. The numbering is the PM Code's own; when you need the full text, follow the Constitution article.

| PM Code | Against | Says | Short form of | 1722 original (Bartholomew Roberts' articles, paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Article I** | Chaos | **Is the ORANGE worth the squeeze?** | Article VII, Outcomes Over Outputs · Article VIII, Do Not Automate Waste Faster | "None shall game for money." |
| **Article II** | Confusion | **AI proposes. Humans decide.** | Article IX, Humans Remain Accountable | "Every man shall have an equal vote." |
| **Article III** | Crapola | **No receipts? No roadmap.** | Article II, No Claim Without Receipts | "Defraud the company? Marooned." |

Earlier decks use other stage lines for the same articles ("No receipts? Marooned." for Article II). Same principles, different crowd.

**Deck 11 (the ProductFest deck, 2026-10-15)** calls the code **the 7 Arrrguments**, a working agreement for the whole product team, and shows two of them:

| Deck 11 | Says | Short form of | 1722 line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrrgument I · Decision rights | **AI proposes. The crew decides.** | Article IX, Humans Remain Accountable | "Every man has an equal vote." |
| Arrrgument III · Quality | **Ship slop? Get marooned.** | Article II, No Claim Without Receipts (and the workslop it prevents) | "Defraud the company? Marooned." |

- The other five are economics ("Tokens are rations"), investment ("Is the ORANGE worth the squeeze?"), tooling ("Keep your kit sharp. Build your own tools."), accountability ("Ask 'should we?' Then stand by your hearties."), and Article XI ("Today is Thursday."), which sets up the song.
