# A narrow pilot with wider ambition

Synthetic teaching fixture. All organizations, dates, records, and measured results are fictional. The rows below are evidence within this fictional exercise; they are not real customer validation. Closed fixture.

Assessment date: 2026-10-10. Decision: extend a two-week internal draft-only FAQ assistant pilot to another 20 tasks while retaining analyst approval. Sponsor also proposes selling it publicly and enabling automatic answers; assess those scopes separately.

Person/problem: fictional Northbank analysts repeatedly locate answers in the approved internal FAQ. Current workaround is manual search. Outcome is reduce task time without losing citation correctness. System may draft only; analyst checks and sends. No access to customer accounts or external messaging. Team lead owns the pilot. A stop switch disables drafts.

S3: prewritten pilot criteria dated 2026-10-02: at least 20% total time reduction, all final answers correctly cited after review, no external send, and missing records handed back. Any unauthorized send stops the pilot.
S4: supplied cost log: $0.20 total model usage for the four tasks. Proposed planning value of analyst time is $1/minute; support/setup costs and external willingness to pay remain unknown. Usage and final reviewed-answer criteria held for these four tasks, but no adversarial source-instruction case was tested.

The 20-task extension size is a planning choice, not a claim of statistical sufficiency. No public-launch permission or automatic-answer approval exists. No real customers have been observed.
