## 4. Tier findings

**Chaos: Is it real, and is it still true?**

Origins collapsed: launch-week materials from Beigeworks: press release, sales deck, LinkedIn post, all-hands slide, CEO keynote, product page, pricing page, release notes, pilot readout. These are not independent proof of market truth.

Stale items and refresh: C3 is explicitly stale because the benchmark score came from last year’s retired model. C2 and C4 are unopened because the named study and analysts are absent.

Receipts rating: **Mystery Meat** for C1-C5 as a launch-week evidence pile. C10 is the only claim with a supplied path to inspectable records.

Sell-By rating: **Mystery Meat** overall; **Total Crapola for reuse of C3 as current evidence** because the supplied text says the underlying model has been retired.

**Confusion: What do we actually know, and does it apply to us?**

Load-bearing assumptions: C6 assumes unnamed customers love it. C8 assumes 100% adoption on day one. C7 assumes “entire discovery process” is one replaceable thing.

Decisive context gaps: user segment, workflow, baseline, owner when wrong, customer economics, Beigeworks economics, adoption ramp, and risk controls are missing for most claims.

Labels rating: **Mystery Meat** for C6 and C7 because the meaningful claim cannot be separated from hype without more definition.

Context rating: **Real Imitation Baloney** for C8 because there is at least an ROI model, but its adoption premise makes the current conclusion unreliable.

**Crapola: Would reality disagree?**

Squares tested or to test: C9 has a demo, but needs a representative customer-data test. C10 has supplied pilot evidence for a narrower workflow with human approval.

Feasible-only flags: C9. A demo proves something can be made to work in demonstration conditions; it does not prove users should trust no-review auto-closing.

Taste rating: **Mystery Meat** for C9; **Mostly Meat** for C10 at the bounded pilot scope, pending inspection of the described logs.

Weakest consequential finding: C9’s no-human-review auto-close claim. The baloney is caught in **Crapola / Usable and Viable squares** because confidence exceeds evidence and the harm of wrong closure is unresolved.
