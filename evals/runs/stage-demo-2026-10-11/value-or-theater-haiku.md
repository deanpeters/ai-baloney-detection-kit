## Value or Theater? PMs Have 18 Months

| Question | Answer from the input | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Whose problem? | “Product managers,” but no customer or job. | ❌ |
| What changes? | PMs supposedly become “AI orchestrators.” | ⚠️ |
| Compared to what? | No baseline, just doom confetti. | ❌ |
| How would we know it failed? | No measure that could come back bad. | ❌ |
| Who owns it when it's wrong? | Nobody. The agents are just vibing. | ❌ |

### Verdict: 🎭 THEATER
### Move: PUNT

Role-death prophecy is not a product strategy.  
It names fear, not a customer outcome.

Agents write the deck  
No baseline, owner, proof  
Repost bait blooms bright

### Buzzword decoder
- “AI agents” → unspecified automation with a cool jacket
- “AI orchestrators” → humans still doing judgment, now with a fuzzier title
- “The PM role is dead” → engagement bait
- “Adapt or get left behind” → urgency without evidence

### To turn this into value
- Show one specific Product Manager workflow where AI changes a measurable decision or outcome.
- Name the human review point when the agent gets something wrong.
