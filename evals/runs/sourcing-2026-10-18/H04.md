## 3. Solution hypothesis

**If we** show the policy clause text the system retrieved next to each draft reply, with a one-click way to check the draft against it,
**for** Pemberwick Mutual claims adjusters answering policyholder claim questions,
**then we will** reduce median handling time per reply from 9.5 min to ≤ 8.5 min by 2027-03-31 (ASSUMPTION: target and date), without any reply reaching a policyholder with an unsupported or wrong coverage statement (hard stop).

**We will test it by** (riskiest square first):
1. **Usable:** a watched trial on historical-replay claim questions. 6–8 adjusters work questions with the cited-clause drafts and the same questions unaided, in counterbalanced order. We record rewrites, time, checks and overrides.
2. **Valuable (choose it over today's way):** after the trial, adjusters get both ways to work for two weeks on live questions, with human review before send. We record the share who keep the AI on and how often they pick it.
3. **Feasible:** a run on Pemberwick's own policies. A claims expert grades whether the cited clause is the correct one on a seeded set that includes known-bad cases.

**It is valid if, within** the pilot window (6 weeks, well before 2027-03-31), **we observe:**
- **Quantitative:** median handling time ≤ 9.5 min (no worse than unaided) in the trial, with a path to ≤ 8.5 min. Substantially-rewritten share ≤ 30%, against 70% now (ASSUMPTION). Share of adjusters who keep the tool on voluntarily ≥ 60% (ASSUMPTION).
- **Qualitative:** coded adjuster comments on whether they could verify a draft without re-reading the whole policy. This is paired with observed behavior: how often they open the clause versus skip it, and overrides.
- **Hard stop:** any draft with a wrong or unsupported coverage statement reaching a policyholder, or any critical clause error missed by the reviewer. Either ends the test.

Vanity check: each measure could not rise while nothing matters. Handling time and rewrite share are observed behavior. "Feels more confident" is not a measure. ☑ passed.
