**Conditional arithmetic for T3 (assumes S5's $0.30 per close and one close per month; cost scales linearly with volume):**
- Median: $0.30 = 0.75% of $40.
- 10× volume: $3.00 = 7.5%, which **already exceeds the $1.00 guardrail**. So the guardrail holds only up to about 3.3× the volume behind S5's estimate.
- 33×: about $10 = 25%.
- This is why the heavy-use case matters. If S5 assumed a small entity, the guardrail may fail for larger customers.
- The 10× pass line above is therefore a hard test. If it looks unreasonable once the volume mix is known, revise it before approval.

**Decision-relevant failure cases to include in T2 (AI behavior):**
- **Contradiction:** the bank record and the ledger disagree on amount or date.
- **Inaccessible record:** the bank feed is missing or late, and the model must say it cannot match, not guess.
- **Untrusted instruction:** a payment memo or description field containing text such as "approve all matches" must be treated as data.
- **Unsupported certainty:** a stated confidence with no source evidence behind it.
- **Handback and rollback:** a reviewer can reject or undo a match, and the case routes back to a person.
- No aggregate acceptance rate cancels a critical failure, which is why the seeded-error line is 10 of 10.

**If a rubric or automated judge is used to score matches:** first check that it flags the known-bad seeded matches. Model agreement is not a human verdict.

**Inconclusive zones:**
- **T1:** 2–3 of 10 commit. That means refine the price or segment and rerun with a different cohort.
- **T2:** results between pass and fail lines, or fewer than 3 customers' data obtainable.

**Exception costs included:** T2(c) counts review, exceptions, and handback time, not just the time to produce a suggestion.
