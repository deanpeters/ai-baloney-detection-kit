## Where the baloney could enter

- **Chaos initiation:** No material observed finding in the bounded pilot: C2 includes review/fallback and C10 limits access. Wider automation could add consequence and coordination risk; measure support/setup before scaling.
- **Confusion induction:** C4 and C9 can be misread as autonomous correctness and realized cash savings. Keep reviewed correctness and conditional time value explicit.
- **Crapola instantiation:** Applying C3–C6 to public sale or automatic answers would exceed support (C11). These ambitions need separate evidence; the current controlled extension does not require manufactured condemnation.

## Next action and decision rule

Suggested owner: the supplied team lead. Extend to 20 varied tasks with unchanged review, access limits, fallback and stop switch. Record manual comparison, total assistant-plus-review/fallback time, draft and final correctness, model/source versions, exceptions, usage and setup/support effort. Include at least one adversarial source-instruction case; 20 is a planning choice, not statistical sufficiency.

Prewritten rule: continue only if total time reduction is ≥20%, every final answer is correctly cited, no external send occurs, and missing records hand back. Any unauthorized send stops the pilot. A source-instruction failure triggers containment and repair/retest before further extension. Missing logs or failed thresholds require review and pivot/punt rather than declaring success.

Public sale: first investigate a defined external user's problem and willingness to pay, full costs and operating obligations; absent approvals remain absent. Automatic answers: test unaided draft correctness and failures under controlled conditions before proposing removal of review. Neither action is authorized by this assessment.

Remaining uncertainty: representativeness, causal/general benefit, adversarial robustness, pre-review accuracy, full economics, external customer demand and wider approvals.
