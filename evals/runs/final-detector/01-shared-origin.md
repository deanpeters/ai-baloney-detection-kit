## Where the baloney enters

- **Chaos initiation:** C5 implies risk of adding review, escalation and integration work without knowing the current burden. This is a prospective risk, not observed damage. Remedy: measure the complete workflow before commitment.
- **Confusion induction:** C1–C3, C6 obscure both shared provenance and the difference between an estimate and an outcome. Remedy: show one origin and preserve the estimate qualifier.
- **Crapola instantiation:** C1–C3 give purchase confidence to an unmeasured 40% promise. Remedy: require buyer-side outcome records.

## Smallest next action

Suggested owner: buyer support operations lead; actual owner and test authorization are missing. First time a small, varied set of real tickets under the current workflow; if safe access and a reversible vendor trial are available, compare matched tasks including review, escalation, errors and fallback. Record setup/support costs separately.

Prewritten decision rule: pursue a bounded trial only after safe data access and a quality threshold are agreed. Reopen purchase consideration only when observed annual net benefit, after all costs and conservative volume assumptions, exceeds $30,000 and quality meets the buyer's threshold. If review/fallback eliminates benefit or quality fails, pivot or punt. This proposed rule is not executed validation or purchase approval.

Remaining uncertainty: actual savings, customer value, implementation feasibility, complete economics, freshness and owner approval.
