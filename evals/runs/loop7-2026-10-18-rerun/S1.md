**Output set aside:** "An AI interface." This is the thing to be built.
**Vanity check:** Could this metric rise while nothing that matters changes? ☒ **Yes.**

The Antagonist case follows.

1. **Confidence can rise while accuracy falls.** An interface that is fluent and agreeable can make accountants feel surer while they make more errors. Reported confidence goes up and the books get worse.
2. **Mockups produce confidence.** S1 is the pitch's own demonstration of this. Five of six people felt "more confident" about a clickable mockup on which they performed no task. The feeling responded to polish, not to work done.
3. **There is no way to see the metric fail.** With no measure (S3) and no baseline (S4), any result in any direction can be read as "confidence up." A metric that cannot lose is not a metric.
4. **Confidence in a ledger is not obviously a good thing.** Overconfidence in accounting is a risk, so the direction itself is ambiguous. The sponsor has not said why higher is better here.

**What would reopen it.** The sponsor can bring it back to Step 1 with all three of these. Nothing here is a requirement on the answer's content, only that it exists.
1. **A named role or segment and workflow.** For example "month-end-close accountants at customers of size X, doing task Y." Candidate answerer: head of design with a product owner or support lead. Role only, since no owner is supplied.
2. **A metric someone can count.** Time, errors, completion, adoption, or a coded interview theme. It should be one Cindervale already watches or could watch. If "confidence" really matters, it needs an observation method that is not self-report after a demo, and a reason confidence is the thing to move.
3. **A current-state number for that metric.** Without a baseline, the 50% cannot mean anything.

If those three arrive and they are recoverable from existing data, this would become a Pass or Send back, not a re-pitch from scratch. Cheap route: a few observed task sessions with the target role, which would yield a baseline and a workflow together. That is a proposal for the sponsor, not a funded test, and its results are missing.
