## 5. Survivor: Four-Risk Grid And Decision Record

### R05: Smart Help-Doc Search

| | For them | For us |
|---|---|---|
| **Should we?** | **Valuable: earned.** Q3 analysis says 38% of tickets are how-to questions already answered in docs; test produced +12% deflection. | **Viable: earned for bounded Q1 investment.** Cost threshold was ≤$0.01/query; actual was $0.004/query. Customer-side risk checked by CSAT staying flat. |
| **Can we?** | **Usable: earned for 10% traffic scope.** CSAT did not decline during the 2-week test. | **Feasible: earned for bounded expansion.** A live 10% traffic test ran from 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-29 and met prewritten thresholds. |

**Move:** Pursue  
**Scope:** Bounded Q1 investment in smart help-doc search, not full irreversible rollout.  
**Deciding evidence:** Prewritten thresholds from 2026-09-10 were met: deflection +12%, CSAT flat, cost $0.004/query.  
**Suggested owner role:** Product owner for self-service/support experience, paired with Support Operations and Engineering.  
**By when:** Before Q1 funding is finalized; re-check after a larger traffic slice or material docs/model/cost change.  
**What would change the call:** CSAT decline, deflection below threshold on broader traffic, cost increase above threshold, or evidence that deflected tickets become worse downstream issues.  
**Where it goes next:** Truth for a bounded expansion/pilot.

**Truth statement:** For Marrowgate’s help-doc search use case, under the tested 10% traffic conditions and current cost structure, the bet has earned bounded Q1 investment. It has not earned a blanket conclusion that all support automation is ready.
