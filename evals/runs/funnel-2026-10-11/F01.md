**Confusion: What do we actually know, and does it apply to us?**

- ASSUMPTION: Copperline’s relevant workflow is PRD/spec drafting, not all Product Manager work.
- ASSUMPTION: All 60 seats would be paid annually, whether or not all users draft specs regularly.

Decisive context gaps:
