## 4. Findings by scope

### Scope 1: 20-task internal draft-only extension with analyst approval retained

**Rating:** Mostly Meat  
**Reason:** The narrow pilot met its prewritten criteria: 33.3% total time reduction, all final reviewed answers correctly cited, missing records handed back, no external send, low model usage cost, and a stop switch. The support/setup cost gap and small sample keep this from Real Deal.  
**Supporting claims:** C2-C17, C20  
**Material blocker:** none for a bounded 20-task extension; support/setup cost and broader task coverage remain bounded gaps.  
**Evidence that would change the rating:** failure on the next 20 tasks, uncited final answers, analyst rejection, unauthorized send, degraded FAQ freshness, support/setup costs overwhelming time savings, or adversarial instruction failure.

### Scope 2: Public launch

**Rating:** Mystery Meat  
**Reason:** The public-launch evidence is missing: no public-launch permission, no real customer observation, no external willingness-to-pay evidence, no support/setup economics, and no adversarial source-instruction test. The internal pilot evidence does not carry that far.  
**Supporting claims:** C18, C19, C21, C23  
**Material blocker:** Valuable, Viable, Usable, and Feasible are all missing or weak for public launch.  
**Evidence that would change the rating:** public-launch approval, real customer tests, willingness-to-pay evidence, support/setup cost model, adversarial testing, external messaging controls, and launch-specific governance.

### Scope 3: Automatic answers / automatic sending

**Rating:** Total Crapola for the current automatic-answer proposal  
**Reason:** The tested system is explicitly draft-only with analyst review. Draft quality before review is not recorded, no automatic-answer approval exists, and unauthorized send stops the pilot. That does not mean automation is impossible someday; it means this proposal has not earned it.  
**Supporting claims:** C2, C11, C14, C19, C22  
**Material blocker:** Usable and Feasible fail for automatic answers under the supplied evidence.  
**Evidence that would change the rating:** recorded pre-review answer quality, explicit approval for automatic-answer scope, source-instruction/adversarial testing, safe-send rules, rollback controls, exception handling, and a human decision rule for when automation is allowed.
