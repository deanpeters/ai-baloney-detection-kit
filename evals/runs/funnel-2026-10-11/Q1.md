## Sorting: Senior Product Manager, Platform

### The grid
|  | Anyone could do it | Only you could do it |
|---|---|---|
| **Moves an outcome** | 🥱 BEIGE → PIVOT (3) | 🎯 TRUTH → PURSUE (4) |
| **Moves nothing** | 🗑️ BUSYWORK → PASS-OFF (3) | 🎭 THEATER → PUNT (2) |

### 🗑️ Busywork → PASS-OFF  (hand it to the robot)
- Summarize customer interview notes and support tickets: needed, but the first draft does not need your hours.
- Build competitive feature comparison matrices: useful scan work, mostly pattern-finding and formatting.
- Own release notes and internal launch announcements: important communication, but much of it is repeatable packaging.

### 🥱 Beige → PIVOT  (add judgment until it's yours)
- Write PRDs and user stories: beige if it is template filling; valuable only when it names the tradeoff.
- Run weekly backlog grooming and sprint planning: beige if it is Jira traffic control.
- Produce the monthly roadmap deck for leadership: beige if it just restates status in prettier clothes.

### 🎭 Theater → PUNT  (cancel the show)
- Track OKRs and report status weekly: theater if the numbers do not change decisions.
- Attend industry conferences and represent the product: theater if it is visibility without learning, deals, or sharper strategy.

### 🎯 Truth → PURSUE  (do more of this)
- Interview customers to understand freight broker workflows: you learn the messy reality the model cannot infer.
- Decide what we won't build, and defend it to sales: this is judgment under pressure.
- Partner with engineering on technical tradeoffs: platform value lives in the tradeoffs, not the tickets.
- Align stakeholders across sales, CS, and finance: only you can broker the real constraints between these people.

### ⚖️ Swing items
- Write PRDs and user stories: beige when they document obvious requirements, truth when they capture a hard customer or architecture decision.
- Produce the monthly roadmap deck: beige when it reports activity, truth when it forces a choice.
- Summarize customer interview notes and support tickets: beige when it condenses text, truth when it spots the pattern that changes the roadmap.

### Hired · Fired · Promoted
- **Hired for:** your Truth square: customer judgment, platform tradeoffs, and the courage to say what not to build.
- **Fired for:** the theater you keep performing: status rituals and visibility work that nobody uses to decide anything.
- **Promoted for:** what you pass off: the hours you free from summaries, matrices, and announcements, spent on customers, tradeoffs, and hard alignment.
