# Proposal: AI first-draft replies for Tier 1 billing tickets

**Problem:** Small-business owners waiting on billing questions (refund status,
invoice copies, plan changes) wait a median 19 hours for a first reply. Billing
tickets are 41% of Tier 1 volume. CSAT on these tickets is 3.1/5 vs 4.2 overall.

**Change:** The model drafts a reply from the account's billing history. An
agent reviews, edits, and sends. Nothing goes out unreviewed.

**Baseline:** Today agents write from scratch using 14 macros.

**Measures:** Median time to first reply (target under 4h), CSAT on billing
tickets, and the share of drafts sent with no edits. If more than 10% of drafts
contain a factual billing error in the 4-week pilot, we stop.

**Owner:** Maria Okafor, Support Ops lead. Reviewed weekly with Finance.
