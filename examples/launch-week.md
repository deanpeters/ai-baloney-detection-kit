# Launch Week at Beigeworks, Inc.: ten claims about "AgentOmatic 3000"

Pulled from one week of Beigeworks' press release, sales deck, LinkedIn posts and all-hands slides. Pour them all in.

1. "AgentOmatic 3000 is the world's first truly agentic product platform." (press release)
2. "A recent Stanford study shows teams using AI agents ship 10x faster." (sales deck; no title, author, link, or date for the study)
3. "AgentOmatic 3000 scored 94% on the industry's top reasoning benchmark." (sales deck; the score is from last year's model, which has since been retired)
4. "Analysts agree agentic platforms are the future of product management." (LinkedIn post; no analyst is named)
5. "AgentOmatic 3000 cuts PRD writing time by 40%." (all-hands slide; source: Beigeworks' own Q3 timesheets for its six in-house PMs. Which customers, which kind of PRD, and compared with what aren't stated)
6. "Customers love it." (CEO keynote; no customers are named)
7. "AgentOmatic 3000 replaces your entire discovery process." (product page; the features are real and shipping: it drafts interview guides and summarizes transcripts. Whose discovery process, which steps it replaces, and who owns the result aren't stated)
8. "AgentOmatic 3000 will pay for itself in one quarter." (pricing page; the ROI model assumes 100% adoption on day one)
9. "AgentOmatic 3000's backlog-triage agent auto-closes duplicate tickets with no human review." (release notes; it works in the demo. In the September beta, 4 of 5 customer teams switched auto-close off within a week, after it closed tickets that weren't duplicates)
10. "In a four-week pilot with three named customer teams, AgentOmatic 3000 cut duplicate-ticket triage from about 6 hours a week to about 1, with a human approving every close." (pilot readout; the customers are named, and the logs are attached)
