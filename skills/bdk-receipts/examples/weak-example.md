# Weak Example: Corroboration by Repetition

**Anti-pattern. Synthetic.** Same inputs as the [worked example](worked-example.md). Every field is filled in, it is tidy, and it is wrong. It counts reposts as independent evidence and lets "faster drafting" become "productivity."

## Sources

| Source | Supports |
|---|---|
| Trade blog | 40% productivity gain ✅ |
| Newsletter | 40% productivity gain ✅ |
| Vendor press release | 40% productivity gain ✅ |
| PM survey | PMs want this ✅ |

**Independent confirmations:** 3

## Rating

**Show Me the Receipts: Mostly Meat.** The 40% figure is well corroborated across three independent publications, and our own survey confirms demand.

**Exit:** Pass, so proceed to contract review.

---

## Why this is weak

- **Three links, one origin.** S1 and S2 cite S3. The independent count is one, and that one is the seller.
- **The claim mutated.** The release measured drafting speed in 12 internal users; the ledger says "productivity."
- **Survey misread.** The survey shows pain with spec writing, not demand for this product.
- **Nothing was inspected,** yet every row has a check mark.
- **Wrong tier's job.** It passes the claim straight to contract review, skipping freshness, context, and any test.

A reviewer should catch it with one question: *"Which of these three would still exist if the press release didn't?"*
