# Weak Example: The Summary

**Anti-pattern. Synthetic.** Same press release as the [worked example](worked-example.md). It is short, fair-sounding, and complete, and it is a summary, not a slice.

## Claims

1. AutoPilot PM is an agentic AI product manager that boosts team productivity by 40%.
2. It is more agentic than previous versions.
3. It democratizes product judgment for forward-thinking organizations.

**Assessment:** The claims are ambitious but plausible given current AI trends. Recommend a pilot.

---

## Why this is weak

- **Bundled claims.** Line 1 packs a capability, an outcome, and a number into one sentence, so the existence of the product lends credibility to the 40%.
- **The source disappeared.** "According to an internal study," "early adopters report": the parts that tell the funnel where to look were cut.
- **Adjectives became claims.** "More agentic" and "democratizes product judgment" can't come back false, yet they're listed as claims.
- **"Talks to customers" vanished,** and it was the claim with the biggest accountability question.
- **It judged.** "Plausible given current AI trends" and "recommend a pilot" are funnel work, done here without a receipt. A utility hands off; it doesn't decide.

A reviewer should ask: *"Which line here could come back false, and what would we check?"*
