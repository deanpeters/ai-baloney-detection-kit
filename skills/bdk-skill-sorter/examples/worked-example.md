# Worked Example: Senior PM, Platform

**Synthetic.** Input: [the fictional Acme Logistics Cloud job description](../../../examples/jd-senior-pm.md). This shows the shape of a good projector-ready run; a live run will word things differently.

## Sorting: Senior PM, Platform (Acme Logistics Cloud)

### The grid
|  | Anyone could do it | Only you could do it |
|---|---|---|
| **Moves an outcome** | 🥱 BEIGE → PIVOT (3) | 🎯 TRUTH → PURSUE (4) |
| **Moves nothing** | 🗑️ BUSYWORK → PASS-OFF (3) | 🎭 THEATER → PUNT (2) |

### 🗑️ Busywork → PASS-OFF  (hand it to the robot)
- Release notes and internal launch announcements: nobody would notice if the robot drafted them
- Summarizing interview notes and support tickets: the summary is free now; the reading isn't
- Weekly OKR status report: assembly, not judgment

### 🥱 Beige → PIVOT  (add judgment until it's yours)
- PRDs and user stories: a template fill is free; the tradeoff nobody else saw is not
- Competitive feature matrices: any AI makes the grid; only you know which row matters to freight brokers
- Sprint planning: anyone can sequence tickets; deciding what the sprint is *for* is yours

### 🎭 Theater → PUNT  (cancel the show)
- Monthly roadmap deck for leadership: who's this for, the customer or the audience?
- Conference attendance "to represent the product": if nobody were watching, would we still go?

### 🎯 Truth → PURSUE  (do more of this)
- Interviewing customers about freight broker workflows
- Deciding what we won't build, and defending it to sales
- Technical tradeoffs with engineering
- Aligning sales, CS, and finance on what matters

### ⚖️ Swing items
- PRDs: beige when they restate the ticket, truth when they record the hard "no"
- Roadmap deck: theater when it's a status pageant, truth when it forces a decision
- Interview summaries: busywork when you only read the AI summary, truth when you were in the room

### Hired · Fired · Promoted
- **Hired for:** your Truth square: you know how a freight broker's week actually goes
- **Fired for:** the theater you keep performing: a monthly deck that never changed a decision
- **Promoted for:** what you pass off: the six hours a week of status and summaries, spent with customers instead

### Monday
- **Pass it off (Feasible):** automate the weekly OKR status email
- **Call one customer (Valuable):** a broker who churned last quarter: what made them leave?
- **Watch one person use it, and shut up (Usable):** sit with a dispatcher using the load board for 30 minutes
- **Say the hard "why not" (Viable):** ask finance which platform feature loses money per customer

---

**Why this is good:** every item has a square, a reason, and a move. Truth is specific to *this* role's customers. The swing items teach the lesson, and Monday is four things you can do this week.
