## Why this is weak

- **It never asked the two questions.** "Could anyone do it?" and "does it move an outcome?" would have moved most of this list.
- **Everything is the good square.** If every item is Truth (or "Leverage"), the grid sorted nothing.
- **No theater, ever.** Every role has some. Saying none is itself theater.
- **No moves.** A square without its move (pass-off, pivot, punt, pursue) is a label, not advice.
- **Invented statistics.** "47% by 2028" and "3x" come from nowhere. The rules forbid forecasts.
- **Monday is a poster.** "Stay curious" isn't something you can do on Monday. "Call the broker who churned" is.
- **It used the old word.** The square is **Truth** now (formerly Leverage).
