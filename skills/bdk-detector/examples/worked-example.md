# Worked Example: Twelve Roadmap Ideas, One Truth

**Synthetic.** Marrowgate Software and its roadmap are invented. From [funnel case 09](../../../evals/loop-cases/loop-09-roadmap-funnel.yaml).

**What went in:** the twelve items on a Q1 slide titled "Our AI Roadmap," each with the one evidence line the team has. · **Decision:** which deserve Q1 investment · **As of:** 2026-10-18 · **Mode:** Context Dump, unattended

## The funnel at a glance

| Stage | Count | Claims |
|---|---|---|
| Entered | **12** | R01–R12 |
| Routed to the work family | 1 | R04 |
| **Chaos**: left | 6 | at Outcomes: sifted out R01, R02, R09 · sent back R07 · at Receipts: sifted out R06, R11 |
| **Confusion**: left | 2 | at Sell-By: sent back R03, R12 |
| **Crapola**: left | 2 | pivot R08 · held for a test R10 |
| **Considered**: reached **Truth** | **1** | R05 |

1 + 6 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 12.

## One line per claim

| ID | Claim | Left at | Exit | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| R07 | Churn prediction | Outcomes | Send back | A model, not an outcome: what changes when a flag fires, and who acts? Customer success can answer |
| R06 | Pricing optimizer "boosts revenue 20%" | Receipts | Sifted out | Four sources, one origin: a vendor webinar |
| R11 | "AI replaces tier-1 support by Q3" | Receipts | Sifted out | One vendor pitch behind a consequential workforce claim |
| R03 | Meeting notes "save 5 hours a week" | Sell-By | Send back | Real receipt, 2023 data, retired model; refresh it rather than kill it |
| R12 | Internal code-review bot, −15% PR cycle time | Sell-By | Send back | Our own trial, but on a model and plugin since replaced; re-run it |
| R08 | Onboarding assistant | Taste Test | Pivot | Valuable earned (8/10 struggle); Usable failed (3/5 won't accept auto-configured permissions). Show changes before applying them, re-test |
| R10 | Contract summarizer | Taste Test | Held for a test | Valuable weak (3 requests); Viable missing ($0.60 a document, no price). Test willingness to pay with the 3 requesters |
| R05 | Smart help-doc search | Considered | **Pursue → Truth** | All four squares earned at 10% traffic against prewritten thresholds |

**R05's outcome statement:** Reduce how-to tickets ≥10% (deflection) by end of Q1 for Marrowgate help-desk customers' end users in self-service support, without lowering CSAT.

## Survivor: R05 smart help-doc search

| | For them | For us |
|---|---|---|
| **Should we?** | **Valuable: earned.** 38% of tickets are how-to questions already answered in docs | **Viable: earned.** $0.004 per query vs a ≤$0.01 threshold; deflection reduces ticket cost |
| **Can we?** | **Usable: earned.** CSAT flat while deflection rose | **Feasible: earned.** Ran on our docs and traffic for two weeks |

**Recommendation:** Pursue: so much proof, at this size, that the planned next step goes forward as planned. **Decision record:** scoped to expanding from 10% to 50% of traffic with the same thresholds, written before results. Owner role: support product lead. Re-check triggers: model price change, any docs restructure. Not earned: 100% rollout before the 50% step reports.

## Damage if ignored

- **Chaos initiation:** R11 would reorganize a team around a sales pitch.
- **Confusion induction:** R02 and R07 have no outcome, and R07 has no owner.
- **Crapola instantiation:** R01, R06, R09, and R11 carry confidence far beyond any evidence.
