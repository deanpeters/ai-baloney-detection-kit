## Why this is weak

- **Document count is not context.** Eight sources and the decisive gap (cost at the top quartile) is still open.
- **Only one side's ROI.** "Customers save 6 hours" is the customer's ledger. Ours was summarized as "within cost" with no arithmetic.
- **The average hides the losers.** At the top quartile each seat costs about $39 to serve against $15 revenue.
- **A perishable price treated as permanent.** The provider has announced a change.
- **A competitor's pricing page** appears as context with no transfer analysis.

A reviewer should ask: *"Show me the math for our heaviest user."*
