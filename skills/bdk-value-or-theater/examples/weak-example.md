# Weak Example: Vibes In, Verdict Out

**Anti-pattern. Synthetic.** Input: [the fictional InsightGenie pitch](../../../examples/pitch-theater.md). It sounds fair and open-minded, and it fills the gaps with charitable guesses.

## Value or Theater? InsightGenie

| Question | Answer | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Whose problem? | Product teams struggling with alignment | ✅ |
| What changes? | Teams get real-time insights | ✅ |
| Compared to what? | Teams without AI | ⚠️ |
| How would we know it failed? | Alignment would drop | ⚠️ |
| Who owns it when it's wrong? | Probably the product team | ⚠️ |

### Verdict: ⏳ NOT YET
Promising platform with strong alignment results (10x). Worth a demo.

---

## Why this is weak

- **It answered for the pitch.** The input never names a customer, a behavior change, a baseline, a failure measure, or an owner. Each of those is the finding; the weak version invented plausible answers instead.
- **"10x more aligned" was treated as a result.** It's a feeling, self-reported, with no unit.
- **No move.** "Not yet" without PIVOT and the one piece of evidence that would move it is a shrug.
- **No buzzword decoder,** on a pitch that is nearly all buzzwords: "supercharge," "game-changing," "AI-native," "democratize," "future-proof."
- **The right call:** 🎭 THEATER → PUNT. Zero of five answered with specifics.
